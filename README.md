# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales performance and customer trends using SQL and Power BI.

The dashboard helps identify:
- Sales trends
- Top-performing categories
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Regional performance
- Monthly revenue growth

## Problem Statement

The e-commerce business was experiencing inconsistent profitability despite having strong sales volume across multiple product categories and regions.

Management noticed that certain products generated high revenue but contributed very low profit margins due to heavy discounts, shipping costs, and inefficient sales distribution. Additionally, there was no centralized reporting system to monitor monthly sales trends, customer segments, or regional performance.

Because the data was scattered across raw transactional files, business teams struggled to answer critical questions such as:

Which products are driving profit vs only generating revenue?
Which regions are underperforming?
Which customer segments contribute the most value?
How do discounts impact profitability?
What are the monthly sales and profit trends?

This project was created to solve these business challenges through data analysis and interactive dashboard reporting.

## Business Objective

The primary objective of this project was to build a data-driven reporting solution that helps business stakeholders monitor sales performance, profitability, and customer behavior in real time.

Using SQL and Power BI, the project focuses on:

Identifying profit-driving products and categories
Detecting loss-making sales patterns
Tracking monthly revenue and profit trends
Analyzing regional and segment-wise performance
Understanding the impact of discounts on business profitability
Supporting better strategic and operational decision-making

The final dashboard provides a centralized view of business KPIs to help management improve profitability and optimize sales strategies.

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Average Discount %
- Monthly Sales Growth
- Regional Profitability
- Top 10 Profitable Products
- Loss-Making Products
- Customer Segment Contribution
- Category & Sub-Category Performance
- Order Volume Trends

## Business Recommendations
Reduce excessive discounting on low-margin products.
Focus inventory and marketing investment on highly profitable categories.
Investigate underperforming regions with consistently low profit margins.
Create targeted campaigns for high-value customer segments.
Monitor monthly sales fluctuations to improve forecasting and inventory planning.
Use profitability-based analysis instead of revenue-only metrics for business decisions.
